---
name: Completion motion direction
description: The approved production animation treatment for the kiosk completion floor directive.
---

Use a one-time 10-second color pulse on the completion floor directive, with two pronounced scale peaks and coordinated crimson, coral, and deep-crimson color phases across the floor heading.

**Why:** The earlier restrained animation was too subtle to reliably catch patients’ attention. The stronger pulse and color variance were explicitly selected for the production kiosk.

**How to apply:** Keep the animation limited to the completion floor directive, preserve the destination’s readability, do not add sandbox replay controls to the patient UI, and disable the motion under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
